**Automation:** `tests/ui/test_01_home_page.py`  |  **Report:** `reports/home-page/Home Page test <DD-MM-YYYY>_<HH-mm-ss>.html` (all reports: `reports/index.html`)
**Application:** https://testautomationpractice.blogspot.com/  |  **ID prefix:** `TC_HOME_`  |  **Master CSV:** `docs/test-cases/home-page.csv`

## Overview
The home page of the practice site shows the page title, the heading and one block for every practice section. These smoke checks tell us in a few seconds that the site is up and the page is complete.

## Scope and notes
- Only the presence of the page title, heading, section headings and footer links is checked here. What every section does is covered in the other modules.
- The section list is the 19 sections that exist on the site today; a new section on the site will not fail these cases.
